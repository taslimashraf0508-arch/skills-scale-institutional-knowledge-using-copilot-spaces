# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UI/UX Designer

### Role Summary
UI/UX Designers ensure product interfaces are usable, accessible, and meet user needs. They partner with Product Managers and Developers from ideation through release, championing the user experience at every stage.

### Responsibilities
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and deliver ready-to-implement designs
- Champion accessibility and usability standards
- Gather user feedback via research and design validation
- Collaborate closely with Developers on design handoff and implementation
- Participate in design reviews and quality assurance for visual/interaction elements
- Document design systems and component guidelines

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden
- Maintain visual and interaction consistency across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with stakeholders
- Handoff documentation for developers
- User research findings and design rationale
- Collaboration with QA on visual acceptance criteria

---

## Customer Support Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support Representatives act as the voice of the customer, relaying product issues, feature requests, and usability challenges gathered via support channels. They provide critical feedback that informs product decisions and process improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Escalate bugs, critical issues, and feature requests from users
- Advise on documentation, help content, and self-serve resources
- Participate in post-release reviews and retrospectives
- Identify patterns in customer feedback and usability concerns
- Collaborate with QA on bug reproduction and prioritization
- Contribute to release notes and customer communication

### Goals
- Reduce customer support volume through better product design
- Ensure customer voice is heard in product decisions
- Improve time-to-resolution for customer-reported issues

### Typical Communication
- Bug reports and feature request escalation
- Customer feedback summaries and trends
- Post-release support readiness and issue triage
- Retrospectives to capture learnings from customer interactions

---

## Security/Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Leads ensure project deliverables comply with internal standards, regulatory requirements, and security best practices. They proactively identify risks and govern mitigation plans throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security and compliance reviews at critical project phases
- Advise on policy alignment and regulatory requirements (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2, etc.)
- Review threat models and security architecture decisions
- Coordinate with Developers during implementation and code review
- Establish security acceptance criteria and testing requirements
- Lead incident response and post-incident reviews
- Maintain security and compliance documentation

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and compliance violations
- Embed security into the development process early
- Minimize risk to the organization and customers

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and threat assessments
- Risk register updates and mitigation planning
- Compliance checklists and regulatory guidance
- Incident response coordination and post-incident debriefs
